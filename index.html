Questions

Before you order

Something else on your mind? Send it with your order and we will answer when we confirm.

Where is the food cooked?
In a licensed commercial kitchen in Toronto, not a home kitchen. Everything is cooked, chilled and packed in one Friday service.
When do I need to order?
The menu goes live Monday at 9:00 am and orders close Wednesday at 11:59 pm. You can change or cancel until then.
How do I reheat my meals?
Each lid shows the time and temperature. Most meals take 12 minutes in a 180°C oven or about 3 minutes in the microwave. Add the sauce and toppings after heating.
How long do meals keep?
Keep them in the fridge and eat them within three days of pickup. The best before date is on every label.
What about allergies?
Every dish lists soy, sesame, shellfish, peanut, gluten, dairy and egg. Our kitchen handles all of them, so we cannot promise any dish is free of traces. Tell us about allergies in your order.
Where do you deliver?
Friday evening delivery covers downtown and midtown Toronto for a $6.50 fee, free on boxes over $80. Pickup runs Friday 5 pm to 8 pm and Saturday 11 am to 2 pm.
How do I pay?
When we confirm your order by text, we send a payment link for card or Interac e Transfer. Nothing is charged until you confirm.
What if a meal is not right?
Tell us by Sunday with a photo and the next one is on us. No forms, no arguing.
